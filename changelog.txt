v0.2.3
Alongside Spooky Mod's 1.4.4 update, we have added new, exclusive cross-mod content for players to enjoy while playing Spirit Reforged and Spooky together.
Note: Most new content is only accessible while playing both Spirit Reforged and Spooky Mod at the same time. 


- Added two new quest minibosses for Spooky's Little Eye Bounties: The Mummiath and Dhampir
- Added new clubs based on Spooky content
- Added Cartographer Map Pins, Uncommon Pots to many Spooky biomes!
- Added Scaregrass, a spooky alternative to Stargrass found in the Spooky Forest! Scaregrass comes with its own foliage and tree types!
- Added Trellises for Spooky Mod Woods: Spooky Forest Wood, Old Birch
... and much more!

**Spooky Mod**
- Added the Boggy Shipyard, a new **Crossmod Ecotone**.
...and much more!
